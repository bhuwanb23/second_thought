## Decisions already taken (and the reasoning to remember)

### D1 — Do not enter any cluster with more than 20 competitors
**Date:** 1 Nov 2026 · **Status:** standing

`mandate-guardrail` (86), `invoice-ar` (60), `reconciliation-reporting` (51), `dispute-chargeback` (49), `shopping-agent` (47), `agent-trust-identity` (38) are excluded. Not because good products cannot exist there, but because *Innovation* and *differentiation* are scored by judges who will have seen forty of them — and the teardowns show the best entries in those clusters are genuinely excellent.

**Revisit only if** a truly novel mechanic emerges that no cluster keyword captures.

### D2 — The record-keeping pivot
**Date:** 1 Nov 2026 · **Status:** done

The research library was reorganised from a flat `research/` + `data/` layout into ten numbered phase folders, each owning one question. Every tool script was re-pointed. All structured datasets are now regenerated by `tools/build_datasets.py` rather than hand-edited, so the CSVs and workbook cannot drift.

### D3 — EV formula frozen
**Date:** 1 Nov 2026 · **Status:** standing

`judge_score × crowd_factor × intent_mult × feasibility`, defined in `../research/07_concepts/EV_RANKING.md`. **Do not retune the weights to make a preferred concept win.** If a concept needs the formula changed to rank first, that is the signal that it should not rank first.

### D4 — Competitor counts are a floor, not a ceiling
**Date:** 1 Nov 2026 · **Status:** standing

The teardowns revealed that cluster counts understate competition in thin clusters. `storefront-builder` had 12 repos but two of them had fully closed the concept. **When a thin cluster is chosen, read the occupants before committing.**

### D5 — AG Grid is the sponsor target
**Date:** 1 Nov 2026 · **Status:** standing

Five winner slots, $10,000, and every concept in the top four has a natural data surface. APIMatic added as a near-free second pool; Render used for hosting. See `../research/06_sponsors/SPONSOR_STRATEGY.md`.

### D6 — C02 is locked; execution replaces revision

**Date:** 1 Nov 2026. **Status:** binding.

The concept has been through five review rounds. The idea, scope and pitch are frozen at
`LOCK.md`; only two pre-committed tripwires can change anything, and their consequences
were written down **before** the results arrived.

**What was corrected in the final round, and why it matters:**

| Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "PayPal reports missed payments to CRAs where Affirm and Afterpay don't" | **Cut** | Affirm reports Pay in 4 to Experian (Apr 2025) and TransUnion (May 2025). The claim was outdated; the surviving argument (PayPal funds the merchant, so it bears the credit risk) needs no contested data |
| "Volume preserved" | **A stated trade** | A DECLINE is lost Pay Later fee revenue. Saying otherwise invites a judge to doubt the rest |
| "No adverse action" | **Two-mode framing** | Shopper-side advice is the clean posture; merchant-side suppression raises steering questions this build does not claim to resolve. Production needs legal review |
| "Validated" | **"Researched"**, and "tested with five shoppers" after the conversations | The evidence is secondary. Overstating it is the easiest thing to puncture |
| "549 repos, two mention BNPL" | **Internal only** | Our own scan; registrants are not submissions |

**Consequence:** any further objection is logged for after submission, not acted on.
