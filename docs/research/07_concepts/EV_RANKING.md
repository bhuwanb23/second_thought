# 07 — Concept ranking and the EV formula

Data: `CONCEPT_PIPELINE.csv` (24 concepts, regenerated by `tools/build_datasets.py`).

---

## The formula

```
judge_score  = mean(tech_impl, design, impact, innovation, presentation)     # 1–5
crowd_factor = 1 / (1 + competing_entries / 15)                              # 0–1
intent_mult  = 0.6 + 0.1 × intent_fit                                        # 0.7–1.1
EV_index     = (judge_score/5) × crowd_factor × intent_mult × (feasibility/5) × 100
```

**Why each term exists:**

- **judge_score** approximates the five Stage-2 criteria. Equal weight, matching the rules.
- **crowd_factor** is the correction that matters most. With 549 repos in the field, a concept in the `mandate-guardrail` cluster (86 competitors) is near-unwinnable on *Innovation* regardless of quality — and the teardowns show those 86 include some of the best work in the contest. At 86 competitors the factor is 0.148; at 2 competitors it is 0.88. That is a 6× difference in expected value for identical execution.
- **intent_mult** encodes "does this answer something PayPal said out loud." Range is deliberately narrow (0.7–1.1) so a good concept cannot be rescued by narrative alone.
- **feasibility** penalises ambitious concepts that cannot be finished by 13 Nov. A half-built excellent idea scores badly on both *Design* and *Presentation*.

**What the formula deliberately ignores:** market size (judges do not score TAM), novelty to the world (only novelty *within the field* matters), and monetisation.

## Three concepts got worse since the teardowns

The cluster *counts* were always in the formula, but the teardowns in `../03_field/TEARDOWNS.md` revealed how strong the occupants are:

- **C03 (agent-ready store, EV 32.3, down from 40.3)** — `rohith1005H/AgentBaazar` already implements PayPal's **Cart API v1 / Store Sync merchant contract** from a product feed, at protocol level, with buyer charged only on shipment. `RAK2315/shopfront-agent` covers the photos-only seller. The cluster count is 12; two of those twelve have closed the concept.
- **C07 (A2A, 31.3)** — `pact-agentic-commerce` ships a deterministic verifier with 275 checks across 55 deliveries and a performance bond.
- **C20 (escrow, 18.7)** — `ayusharyakashyap/Covenant` compiles a plain-English deal into typed conditions and uses AUTHORIZE correctly. That is the concept, done.

## Two concepts got better

- **C02 (BNPL, 64.5)** — only 3 repos in the cluster and **none appears in the top-depth teardowns**. The consumer-side denial and lockout experience documented in `../04_pain/QUALITATIVE_QUOTES.md` sections D–E appears to be **entirely unaddressed by the field**. Evidence base is strong (LendingTree 47% late, CFPB 63% stacking) and intent fit is high (financial services >20% of transaction margin, growing double digits).
- **C01** remains #1 but its derivative risk is unchanged and real.

## The decision, stated plainly

**If you want the highest expected score with manageable risk: C02.**
It has the strongest combination of sourced evidence, high intent fit, a genuinely open field, and a build scope that fits in eleven days. It also has a natural AG Grid surface (a repayment calendar and exposure view) and a natural demo moment (a checkout where the agent declines to offer Pay Later, and explains why).

**If you want the highest ceiling and accept the risk: C01.**
Best-aligned to the CTO's own demo, best evidence for the *pain*, and the thinnest cluster that matters. But you would be shipping a small version of a product PayPal announced on 10 Aug 2026, and `Thinkelution/paypal-retrofit` already occupies the adjacent space credibly. Only take this if you commit to the **upgrade-and-certify** framing (not connect), and ship a portable certification artefact.

**If you want the biggest human story: C06.**
$144.8B flowing into India, 5.30% average cost vs 3.29% best-in-class, $147/year lost per $400/month sender. But intent fit is 3 — it is not on PayPal's agentic narrative — and remittance is genuinely hard to demo convincingly in sandbox.

Eleven days remain (1 → 12 Nov). The field leaders have been building for weeks and already have videos. **Anything not demoable by 9 Nov is not going to be demoable.** Plan backwards from a recorded video on 10 Nov and a submission on 11 Nov.
